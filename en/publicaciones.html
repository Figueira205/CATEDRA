    Guidelines for authors

    Articles: 6,000-9,000 words, including notes. Critical notes: up to 3,000. Reviews: up to 1,500. Editable file (.docx or .odt), 1.5 line spacing, 12pt body text.

    Chicago (author-date) or full footnotes, used consistently throughout the text. A final bibliography is mandatory for articles.

    Double-blind peer review. Indicative response time: 8-10 weeks. Possible decisions are acceptance, acceptance with revisions, or reasoned rejection.

    Authors retain their rights and authorise open publication under a Creative Commons licence (BY-NC-ND, unless otherwise agreed). No publication fees are charged.

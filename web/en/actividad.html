    Rethinking Hispanidad in the 21st Century

    Opening session of the 2026-2027 academic year: new historiographical approaches to the Hispanic world, with open debate.

    Register →

    The session

    The ongoing seminar is the Chair's space for continuous conversation. This opening session will present the year's programme and discuss new historiographical approaches to the Hispanic world: from Atlantic history to connected histories.

    Programme

    Institutional welcome and presentation of the 2026-2027 seminar sessions. Delivered by the Chair's leadership (speakers to be confirmed from the CMS).

    "Rethinking Hispanidad in the 21st Century": state of the question and lines of debate. Speaker to be confirmed.

    Moderated discussion with in-person and online participation.

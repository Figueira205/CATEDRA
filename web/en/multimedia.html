    The archive grows with each activity. Check back soon or see the agenda.

    Accessibility and rights

    Videos include visible controls and never play sound automatically. Transcripts and subtitles are published alongside each item when available. Credits and usage rights are listed in each item's record; historical images are shown as study material, critically contextualised.

    How the archive is organised

    Each item belongs to the "Multimedia" collection in the CMS, with fields for type, date, event, project, people, credits, rights, transcript and authorised download. The filters on this page are generated from those fields.
